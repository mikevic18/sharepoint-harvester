🌾 OneDrive Harvester
👆 Drag & drop this button onto your browser's Bookmarks bar
  1. Install: Drag the blue button above to your Bookmarks bar (or right-click it → Bookmark this Link).
  2. Navigate: Open any folder in modern OneDrive or SharePoint.
  3. Launch: Click 🌾 OneDrive Harvester in your bookmarks. The Cockpit HUD appears immediately.
  4. Harvest: Click ▶ Start Harvesting. It recursively crawls subfolders, downloads up to 10 GB batches, and tracks progress.
